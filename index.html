📖 Betting Terms
🎯
ML (Moneyline)
Bet on the outright winner of the game. No point spread involved.
📊
Spread
Point handicap. Team must win by X points (or lose by less than X).
⬆️⬇️
Over/Under (O/U)
Total combined score. Bet whether it goes OVER or UNDER the line.
🎰
Parlay
Combined multi-leg bet. All picks must win for payout. Higher risk, higher reward.
👤
Prop Bet
Player or team statistic bet (e.g., Ohtani OVER 1.5 Total Bases).
➡️
PUSH
Tie result. Stake returned, no win or loss.
