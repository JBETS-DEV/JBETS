📚 About Kelly Criterion

The Kelly Criterion is a formula used to determine the optimal size of a bet to maximize long-term growth of your bankroll while minimizing the risk of ruin.

Formula

f* = (bp - q) / b

Where: b = decimal odds - 1, p = win probability, q = 1 - p

Recommendations

  • ⭐ Use Quarter Kelly (25%) for safer bankroll management
  • 📉 Never bet more than 5% of bankroll on single bet
  • 🎯 Be honest about your win probability estimates
  • 💰 Current system uses 0.25 Kelly fraction
