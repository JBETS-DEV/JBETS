📚 Kelly Criterion Explained

What is Kelly? The Kelly Criterion is a formula for optimal bet sizing that maximizes long-term growth while minimizing risk of ruin.


Formula: f* = (bp - q) / b

  • f* = fraction of bankroll to bet
  • b = decimal odds - 1
  • p = probability of winning
  • q = probability of losing (1 - p)

Why Quarter Kelly? We recommend 25% Kelly to reduce variance and protect against estimation errors in probability.
