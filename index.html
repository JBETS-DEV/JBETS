📖 Betting Terms
💰
Moneyline (ML)
Bet on which team wins outright. -150 means bet $150 to win $100.
📊
Spread
Point handicap. -5.5 means team must win by 6+ points.
📈
Over/Under (O/U)
Total combined score. O 224.5 means you bet total exceeds 224.
🎰
Parlay
Multi-leg bet. All picks must win. Higher risk, higher reward.
